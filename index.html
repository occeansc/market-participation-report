Summary

Five calendar years, six markets and three routes in: the journey so far

How it began. The journey started in March 2020, when a first personal account was opened as COVID volatility peaked. After a pause, participation resumed in September 2023 and has been continuous since, so it now spans five calendar years: 2020, and 2023 to 2026.

How it broadened. From 2023 the journey widened beyond currencies (foreign exchange, or FX) to oil and gold, then in 2024 to silver, US index futures, Bitcoin and Nigerian stocks, and in 2025 to US stocks. Three routes in have been used: personal accounts; accounts with proprietary trading firms (“prop firms”), each beginning as a paid evaluation of trading ability; and 1kHooD, a US stock fund run with a limited partner. Together that is 19 accounts and 2,124 trades.

Prop firms. 15 prop-firm accounts were run at a cash cost of $1,016 in fees. They provided $291,000 of account capacity and produced an account-level result of −5.4%. Two stages were passed; no payouts have been received.

The equity funds. Both are active. 1kHooD earned +$681 on $1,000 (68%). The Nigerian stock account, traded on the Nigerian Exchange (NGX), earned ₦92,037 net of fees on ₦150,000 (61%).

Where it stands. As of September 2026 the cash result across all accounts is −$886, up from a low of −$1,499 at the end of 2024; 2025 and 2026 were each positive, led by the two equity funds. The journey drew on personal (24%), friend (49%) and family (27%) capital, of which 61% went into US instruments, 35% into global FX and 4% into Nigerian stocks. ₦400,000 of ₦720,000 has been repaid to one family lender, and 1kHooD has distributed $1,458 to its partners.

7 · Research approach

Pattern recognition is the core thesis; trades per evaluation fell from 164 in 2024 to 27 in 2026

Primary approach. Generating value from the markets through the application of the art, science and technology of pattern recognition.

Art

Reading structure, context and behaviour in price: the discretionary judgement of what a pattern means now.

Science

Testing whether a pattern repeats with an edge: samples, expectancy and risk limits rather than anecdote.

Technology

Encoding what works into indicators, dashboards and automated systems so it can be applied without drift.

Supporting lenses

LensRole
Technical analysisStructure, levels, momentum and reversal signals across FX, futures and stocks
FundamentalsSector and company selection for the equity funds, including thematic sector intelligence
EventsMacro and geopolitical catalysts: rates, elections, wars, supply shocks (see events, page 4)

Evidence from the record

Evaluations in 2024 averaged 164 trades each, and the largest reached 584; in 2026 they averaged 27. Measured against account size, the 9 evaluations of 2024 ended −5.8% and the 6 of 2026 ended −1.3%; 0 of 9 finished positive in 2024 and 2 of 6 in 2026. The samples are small and the firms, account sizes and instruments differ, so this is indicative rather than proof. The equity funds are the profitable strand.

Tooling built along the way

Rule-based systems and dashboards are part of the approach: automated trading rules for two trading platforms, an intraday trading engine, charting indicators, and a sector-intelligence tool.

Notes

Basis of preparation

Cash basis. Personal accounts show cash result; prop firms show the evaluation fee paid; funds show trading result before the general and limited partner split. Evaluation-account profit and loss is memo only.

Currency. Reported in USD. Nigerian stock results are recorded in naira and converted to USD at approximate monthly ₦/$ levels: recorded rates for 2024 and published Central Bank of Nigeria levels for 2025–26 (about ₦1,360–1,530). Family amounts are shown in naira as recorded.

Nigerian stocks. Gains in the trade sheet are before fees; fees are deducted here, giving ₦92,037 net, which agrees with the sheet’s realised total. One open position (6 shares of Geregu Power, bought for ₦5,400) is not yet sold and is excluded; at the price recorded in the sheet it is ₦446 below cost.

Data gaps. Alpari 2020 predates detailed trade records (account-level only). MyFundedFX #2 is partly summarised, MyFundedFX #4 comes from the firm’s performance report, and Alpari 2023 uses cash result (−$324.93) rather than the trade record (−$284.73).

Funding rule. Unattributed funding is personal. No prop payouts, no other accounts, and no participation in 2021–22.

Sources. Account and trade-level portfolio records, Nigerian stock trade records, and the family credit and repayment log. Event dates are from public reporting. Data as of September 2026.

Terms. A prop firm is a proprietary trading firm that lets a trader use its account after passing a paid evaluation. FX is trading one currency against another; futures are standardised contracts on an index or commodity. A CFD is a contract for difference, which tracks a price without owning the asset. NGX is the Nigerian Exchange, where Nigerian stocks are traded. Alpari is the online broker used for the two personal accounts. In 1kHooD, the general partner runs the fund and the limited partner provides the capital.
