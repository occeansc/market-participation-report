What the record shows

Participation came in three waves. A first personal account in the COVID crash (2020), a two-year pause, then personal and prop-firm accounts in 2023–24, and since 2025 the equity funds plus a second prop campaign in 2026.

Most capital came from others. Of $2,846 deployed, 24% was personal, 49% came from friends and 27% from family. 61% went into US instruments, 35% into global FX and 4% into Nigerian equities.

Prop evaluations were the costly way in. 15 evaluations cost $1,016 in fees; 2 stages were passed and no payout has been received. Their −$15,817 of account-level losses was never personal capital, so this report counts the fee, not the notional loss, as the cash cost.

The equity funds are the positive strand. 1kHooD earned +$681 on $1,000 (68%). NGX earned ₦92,037 net of fees on ₦150,000 (61%).

Little has flowed back as capital. ₦400,000 of ₦720,000 has been repaid to one family lender; 1kHooD has distributed $1,458 in profit shares to the general partner and to TA, its limited partner.

Results are shown on a cash basis: personal-account outcomes, prop-firm fees, and fund trading results. Evaluation-account P&L is reported separately as a memo item.

Financial market participation 2020, 2023–2026 · Foabao7 · Research approach

Pattern recognition is the core thesis; trades per evaluation fell from 164 in 2024 to 27 in 2026

Primary approach. Generating value from the markets through the application of the art, science and technology of pattern recognition.

Art

Reading structure, context and behaviour in price: the discretionary judgement of what a pattern means now.

Science

Testing whether a pattern repeats with an edge: samples, expectancy and risk limits rather than anecdote.

Technology

Encoding what works into indicators, dashboards and automated systems so it can be applied without drift.

Supporting lenses

LensRole
TechnicalsStructure, levels, momentum and mean-reversion signals across FX, futures and stocks
FundamentalsSector and company selection for the equity funds, including thematic sector intelligence
EventsMacro and geopolitical catalysts: rates, elections, wars, supply shocks (see events, page 4)

Evidence from the record

Evaluations in 2024 averaged 164 trades each and the largest reached 584; in 2026 they averaged 27. Nine evaluations in 2024 lost $15,549 at account level, against −$267 across six in 2026. The equity funds, where the horizon is longer, are the profitable strand.

Tooling built along the way

Rule-based systems and dashboards are part of the approach: cTrader (C#) and MT5 (MQL5) implementations of five manual strategies, a Python intraday mean-reversion engine, TradingView Pine Script indicators, and a thematic stock-sector web app.

Basis of preparation

Cash basis. Personal accounts show cash result; prop firms show the evaluation fee paid; funds show trading result before the general and limited partner split. Evaluation-account P&L is memo only.

Currency. Reported in USD. NGX is naira-denominated in source and converted to USD at approximate monthly ₦/$ levels: recorded rates for 2024 and CBN NFEM levels for 2025–26 (about ₦1,360–1,530). Family amounts are shown in naira as recorded.

NGX. Trade gains in the source are gross; fees are deducted here. One open position (6 Geregu shares) is unrealised and excluded. The source workbook’s rolled-up gain (₦110,719) sits ₦778 below the trade-level total because three July 2025 rows carry an open mark rather than the exit price.

Data gaps. Alpari 2020 predates journaling (account-level only). MyFundedFX #2 is partly summarised, MyFundedFX #4 comes from the firm’s performance report, and Alpari 2023 uses cash result (−$324.93) rather than the journal (−$284.73).

Funding rule. Unattributed funding is personal. No prop payouts, no other accounts, and no participation in 2021–22.
